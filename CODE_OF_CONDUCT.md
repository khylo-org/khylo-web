# Code of Conduct

Khylo is for everyone who wants to own their media. We want every conversation here, in issues, pull requests and
discussions, to be welcoming and useful.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain things plainly.
- Keep to the topic. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- No sharing of other people's private information.
- No spam, advertising, or promoting piracy. Khylo is for media you own or have the right to use.

## What happens

People who break these rules may have comments removed, be asked to stop, or be blocked from the project. Report a problem
privately through the repository's **Security** tab (Report a vulnerability) if it can't be raised in public, or by contacting a
maintainer through GitHub.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
