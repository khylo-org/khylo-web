# Security

## Reporting a problem

Please report security problems privately, not as a public issue. On GitHub, open this repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version (the build number is shown in the dashboard and in the app's Settings).

We aim to reply within a few days and to fix confirmed problems in the next build.

## What to expect

- Khylo keeps your library, accounts and settings on your own hardware. Nothing is sent to a Khylo cloud.
- Keep your server up to date, and put it behind HTTPS if you expose it to the internet.
